Matsu City

A private business dashboard run by Jay Matsumoto

What it is

Matsu City is Jay Matsumoto's own dashboard for keeping track of his businesses. It is not a public app. Only Google accounts that Jay has approved can sign in: Jay, and Nikk Amstadter, the owner of the YouTube channel Big Bear Weather and More.

What it does with Google

When the channel owner presses Connect YouTube and approves, Matsu City reads the channel's stats with read-only access: video views, watch time, subscribers and similar YouTube Analytics numbers. It shows those numbers on the private dashboard so Jay and Nikk can see how the channel is doing.

It cannot upload, change, delete or comment on anything, and it never sees the Google password.
